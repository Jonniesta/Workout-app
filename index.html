Workout of the day

Build muscle with bodyweight: Stop each set with roughly 1–3 good reps left. For timed holds, record seconds instead of reps. When your top rep goal becomes easy, slow the lowering phase, add a pause, or progress to a harder variation. Warm up 5 minutes first. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets.
